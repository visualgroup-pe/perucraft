The Journey

The essential highlands

Lima, Arequipa, the Colca canyon, Cusco and Machu Picchu — the great highland highlights in ten considered days, closing with a mountain of your choosing.

Finish on the painted slopes of Vinicunca or beside the still, glacial waters of Humantay — whichever calls to you. As ever, the whole journey stays yours to reshape, in pace, comfort and palate.

Day by Day

The itinerary

  1. Day 1

    Arrival in Lima

    A warm welcome to the capital, and time to settle before the days ahead.

  2. Day 2

    Lima, colonial & modern

    A city tour through the historic centre and the elegant coastal districts — the old and the new Lima, side by side.

  3. Day 3

    Lima · Arequipa

    A city tour and the Santa Catalina Monastery in the white city, beneath its ring of volcanoes.

  4. Day 4

    Arequipa · Chivay · Colca Canyon

    Up onto the high plateau and down towards the canyon, with thermal springs at the day's end.

  5. Day 5

    Cruz del Cóndor · Arequipa

    Condors on the morning air at the Cruz del Cóndor, then the return to Arequipa.

  6. Day 6

    Arequipa · Cusco

    On to Cusco, the old Inca capital, and its thin, luminous mountain air.

  7. Day 7

    Cusco & the archaeological park

    A city tour and the ruins above it — Qoricancha, Sacsayhuamán, Qenqo, Puca Pucara and Tambomachay.

  8. Day 8

    Machu Picchu

    A full day at the citadel, by scenic train, with a guided visit.

  9. Day 9

    A mountain of your choosing

    Rainbow Mountain — Vinicunca, the slopes of Ausangate and the village of Cusipata — or Humantay Lake, by way of Mollepata and Soraypampa, with an offering to the earth.

  10. Day 10

    Departure from Cusco

    A last morning in the Andes before your onward journey.

Every day here is a starting point — reshaped, in full, around your budget, your style and your palate.
