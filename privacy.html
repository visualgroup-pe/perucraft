Privacy Policy

Your trust matters to us. This policy explains, in plain terms, how we look after the information you share with Perú Crafted Experiences.

The information we collect

When you enquire about a journey we collect the details you choose to share — your name, contact details and travel preferences — so that we can design and discuss your itinerary.

How we use it

Your information is used solely to plan your travel, respond to your enquiry and, where you have opted in, to send occasional letters from Peru. We never sell your data.

Sharing with partners

To arrange your journey we share only the necessary details with trusted local partners — hotels, guides and transport providers — who are bound to handle your data with equal care.

Your rights

You may ask us at any time to access, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to unsubscribe from our newsletter. Simply get in touch and we will act promptly.

This is a template policy provided with the site and should be reviewed before launch.
