The Journey

The great sweep of Peru

Colonial Lima, the coast at Paracas and the enigma of the Nazca lines, Arequipa and the condors of Colca, Lake Titicaca and, at the last, Cusco and Machu Picchu.

Thirteen days that follow the old sun route up into the Andes, gathering the country's great landscapes and cultures as they climb. Every day remains yours to reshape, in pace, comfort and palate.

Day by Day

The itinerary

  1. Day 1

    Arrival in Lima

    A warm welcome to the capital, and time to settle before the journey ahead.

  2. Day 2

    Lima, colonial & modern

    Huaca Pucllana, the San Isidro olive grove, the Museum of Art, Plaza San Martín, the Plaza de Armas (Cathedral, Government Palace, City Hall), the San Francisco monastery and catacombs, Parque del Amor and Larcomar.

  3. Day 3

    Lima · Paracas · Ica · Nazca

    The Ballestas Islands and "El Candelabro" at Paracas; then Ica, with a wine cellar, the Huacachina oasis, a dune buggy and a sandboard, before continuing to Nazca.

  4. Day 4

    Nazca · Arequipa

    A scenic flight over the Nazca Lines — the Spider, the Hummingbird, the Monkey — with an overnight transfer to Arequipa.

  5. Day 5

    Arequipa

    A city tour and the Santa Catalina Monastery, the La Compañía cloisters, Carmen Alto and the Yanahuara viewpoint, with views of the Misti, Pichu Pichu and Chachani volcanoes.

  6. Day 6

    Arequipa · Chivay · Colca Canyon

    The Pampa Cañahuas Reserve and its vicuñas, Patapampa at 4,800 m and the Andes viewpoint, with thermal springs.

  7. Day 7

    Cruz del Cóndor · Puno

    The Cruz del Cóndor — the villages of Pinchollo, Maca and Yanque, and the Antahuilque and Choquetico viewpoints — before continuing to Puno.

  8. Day 8

    Lake Titicaca

    The Uros floating islands and the island of Taquile.

  9. Day 9

    The Sun Route · Puno to Cusco

    The Pukara lithic museum, the La Raya pass, Raqchi and the temple of Wiracocha, and the Andahuaylillas chapel.

  10. Day 10

    Cusco & the archaeological park

    Plaza Mayor, the Cathedral, Qoricancha, Sacsayhuamán, Qenqo, Puca Pucara and Tambomachay.

  11. Day 11

    The Sacred Valley

    Pisac and its artisan market, Urubamba, Ollantaytambo and Chinchero.

  12. Day 12

    Machu Picchu

    A full day at the citadel, by scenic train, with a guided visit.

  13. Day 13

    Departure from Cusco

    A last morning in the Andes before your onward journey.

Every day here is a starting point — reshaped, in full, around your budget, your style and your palate.
