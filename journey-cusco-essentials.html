Day by Day

The itinerary

  1. Day 1

    Cusco city tour

    Qoricancha, the Temple of the Sun, and the ruins above the city — Sacsayhuamán, Qenqo, Puca Pucara and Tambomachay — ending at the Plaza de Armas.

  2. Day 2

    The Sacred Valley, Maras & Moray

    Chinchero and its ancestral weaving with natural dyes, the Ollantaytambo fortress, the agricultural terraces of Moray, and the Maras salt pans.

  3. Day 3

    Machu Picchu

    The Inca citadel, reached by scenic train, with a guided visit.

Every day here is a starting point — reshaped, in full, around your budget, your style and your palate.
