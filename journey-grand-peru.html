Day by Day

The itinerary

  1. Days 1–2

    Lima

    The historic centre — Plaza Mayor, the Cathedral, the San Francisco monastery and its catacombs — the Larco Museum, the Magic Water Circuit, and the food of Miraflores and Barranco.

  2. Day 3

    Paracas

    The Ballestas Islands, with their sea lions, Humboldt penguins and seabirds, and the wild shores of the Paracas National Reserve.

  3. Day 4

    Ica

    The Huacachina oasis, a dune-buggy ride and sandboarding, and a wine and pisco tasting at a traditional cellar.

  4. Days 5–7

    Arequipa

    The historic centre, the Santa Catalina Monastery, and the Yanahuara and Carmen Alto viewpoints beneath the volcanoes.

  5. Days 8–9

    Colca Canyon

    Condor watching at the Cruz del Cóndor, thermal springs, and an overnight in Chivay.

  6. Days 10–11

    Lake Titicaca

    The floating Uros islands and the island of Taquile, out on the highest navigable water in the world.

  7. Days 12–15

    Cusco & the Sacred Valley

    A city tour and the Sacred Valley — Pisac, Ollantaytambo and the Maras salt pans.

  8. Day 16

    Machu Picchu

    The Inca citadel, with the option of Huayna Picchu or Machu Picchu Mountain.

  9. Days 17–19

    Tambopata

    The Tambopata National Reserve and its wildlife — macaws, monkeys and caimans — and a boat trip on the Madre de Dios river.

  10. Day 20

    Return to Lima

    A final morning before the journey home.

Every day here is a starting point — reshaped, in full, around your budget, your style and your palate.
